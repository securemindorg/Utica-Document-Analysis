Important Privacy & Usage Information

  • Client-Side Processing & Privacy: All uploaded documents and pasted text are processed entirely within your web browser[cite: 12]. No data is sent to an external server or stored anywhere[cite: 12].
  • No AI Used in Analysis: This tool relies exclusively on deterministic statistical algorithms, mathematical formulas (e.g., Shannon Entropy), and explicit pattern matching[cite: 12]. No artificial intelligence or machine learning models are used to evaluate your text[cite: 12].
  • Interpretative Guidelines: Results are designed as baseline indicators to highlight structural patterns and direct further review[cite: 12]. Scores should be interpreted in context rather than taken at absolute face value[cite: 12].
  • Thesis/Dissertation Analysis: Do not take results at face value for full Thesis or Dissertation documents. Due to the technical nature and number of interations, these students have much more advanced styles of writing and may get improperly detected as AI.
